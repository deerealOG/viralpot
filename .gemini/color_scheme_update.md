## Color Psychology Rationale

### Why Orange?
✅ **Energy & Enthusiasm** - Perfect for viral content creation
✅ **Action-Oriented** - Creates urgency and excitement
✅ **Creativity** - Associated with creative thinking
✅ **Social Media Aligned** - Matches platform energy (TikTok, Instagram)
✅ **Attention-Grabbing** - Stands out like viral content should
✅ **Differentiation** - Not overused in SaaS tools

### Why Teal as Complement?
✅ **Color Wheel Complement** - Opposite to orange, creates perfect balance
✅ **Professional** - Adds sophistication to the energetic orange
✅ **Calming** - Balances orange's intensity
✅ **Modern** - Contemporary and fresh
✅ **Readable** - Good for text and UI elements
